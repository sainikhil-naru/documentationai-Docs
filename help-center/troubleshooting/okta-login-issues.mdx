---
title: Cannot Login with Okta
description: Quick fix for Veritent Okta SSO login errors.
---

## Symptoms

- Error: "Unable to authenticate using Okta"
- Stuck on login screen

## Common Causes

- Wrong Okta Org URL
- Revoked or expired Okta token
- Account disabled in Okta

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Okta URL">
    Make sure Okta Org URL matches your company account (e.g. `company.okta.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh API Token">
    Generate a new Okta API token and update in Veritent dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Admin">
    Confirm your Okta account status with your IT admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>
